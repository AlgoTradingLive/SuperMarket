Cancellation & Refund Policy

SM Super Market
Last Updated: August 10, 2026

This policy explains how order cancellations and refunds are handled on the SM Super Market App.

1. Cancelling an Order

You may cancel an order free of charge as long as it is still in the "Placed" status and the store has not started packing it. Once an order moves to "Packed" or a later status, it may no longer be possible to cancel it, as preparation for delivery has already begun. To request cancellation, contact us using the details below as soon as possible after placing the order.

2. Cash on Delivery Orders

For orders paid via Cash on Delivery, no payment is collected until the order is delivered. If you cancel before dispatch, no charge applies. If you refuse delivery without a valid reason (such as a damaged or incorrect item) after the order has already been dispatched, we may restrict future Cash on Delivery orders on your account.

3. Online Payment Orders

For orders paid online (UPI, card, netbanking, etc. where available), if your order is cancelled before dispatch, the full amount will be refunded to your original payment method. Refunds are typically processed within 5–7 business days, though the exact time to reflect in your account depends on your bank or payment provider.

4. Damaged, Incorrect, or Missing Items

If you receive a damaged, incorrect, or expired product, or an item is missing from your order, please contact us within 24 hours of delivery with details (and a photo where applicable). Valid claims will be resolved through a replacement, a refund for the affected item, or store credit, at our discretion.

5. Non-Returnable Items

For hygiene and safety reasons, perishable items (such as fresh produce, dairy, and bakery items) once accepted at delivery generally cannot be returned, except in cases of damage, spoilage, or an incorrect item being delivered.

6. Failed Delivery Attempts

If delivery could not be completed because you were unreachable or unavailable at the provided address, we may attempt redelivery or cancel the order. For Cash on Delivery orders, no charge applies if the order is ultimately cancelled. For prepaid orders, a refund will be processed after deducting any delivery costs already incurred, where applicable.

7. How to Request a Refund or Cancellation

Contact us through the details below with your Order ID and the reason for your request. We aim to respond within 24–48 hours.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last Updated" date above indicates when it was most recently revised.
